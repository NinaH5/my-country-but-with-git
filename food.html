Food

It's a complete waste if you visit and not eat Lebanon's tasty dishes

Tabouleh:

A refreshing green salad featuring finely chopped parsley, tomatoes, mint, onion, and a small amount of bulgur, dressed with lemon and olive oil.

Manakish:

A popular flatbread often topped with a blend of thyme, sesame seeds, and olive oil, or cheese and more, frequently enjoyed for breakfast

Shawarma:

Thinly sliced marinated chicken or meat roasted on a rotating spit and wrapped in flatbread with garlic sauce. Best food in the world!

Kibbeh:

A savory dish made of bulgur and minced meat mixed with spices, shaped into balls or trays and baked or fried.

Fatoush

: A crunchy garden salad mixed with fresh vegetables and crisp toasted pita pieces.
