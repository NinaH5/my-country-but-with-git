There are many incredible places to visit such as:

Beirut:

The capital of Lebanon mixes modern life with rich history, featuring the scenic Beirut Corniche seaside promenade and the National Museum of Beirut.

Jeita Grotto:

A stunning system of two separate, interconnected magnificent underground limestone caves that you can explore on foot and by boat.

Baalbak:

Home to some of the largest and grandest Roman temple ruins ever built, including the massive Temple of Jupiter.

Byblos:

One of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, known for its historic old souk, medieval castle, and ancient port.
